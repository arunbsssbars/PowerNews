Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026 | Last Updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PowerNews ("we", "our", or "us"). PowerNews provides curated power, energy, renewable capacity, discom telemetry, and regulatory intelligence for India across our mobile applications (Android, iOS) and web platform at powernews-app-2026.web.app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account & Authentication Information:

  • When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your verified Google account profile information (your email address, name, and profile photo URL) provided directly by Google Identity Services.
  • When you register via email and password, we collect your email address, display name, and authentication tokens via Google Firebase Authentication.

B. Application Preferences & Usage:

  • Your saved bookmarks, sector reading history, and preferred regional utility filter (e.g., Delhi / NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru).
  • Coarse regional location (determined either via device permission or coarse IP lookup) used strictly to highlight relevant regional discom updates. We do not track fine-grained GPS routes or store continuous background location.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and maintain your user session across devices.
  • To synchronize bookmarked articles and personal executive briefings.
  • To customize power sector news feeds according to selected state discoms or renewable categories.
  • To deliver system notifications regarding breaking energy sector developments.
  • To ensure platform security and prevent unauthorized access or abuse.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We only share minimal technical data with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate PowerNews:

  • Google Identity & Firebase (Google LLC): For secure authentication, token verification, and cloud synchronization.
  • Google Cloud & Render: Cloud hosting for web applications and backend API processing.

5. Data Retention & Security

All client-server communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Inactive articles are automatically aged out in accordance with our 30-day retention policy, while your personal bookmarks remain preserved until you delete them.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion

You retain full control over your personal data. You may:

  • View, export, or clear your locally cached bookmarks directly inside the application.
  • Sign out at any time to remove all local authentication credentials from your device.
  • Permanently delete your account and associated data directly through the in-app Delete Account option in Profile Settings, or by emailing our privacy team.

7. Children's Privacy

PowerNews is intended for energy industry professionals, researchers, students, and general news readers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
